## 🌱 Why This Site Exists

I didn’t begin this journey to become religious.  
I started learning the path of the Buddha because I was struggling—with **anger**, with **emotional exhaustion**, and with the quiet but overwhelming pressure of work and family life.

We recently **welcomed a new baby** into our family. Alongside raising our older son, this brought us great love—and also great responsibility, fatigue, and emotional intensity.

Work pressure was one of the main stressors.  
I work as a **Lead Software Developer** at a company, and the project we were on was not going well.  
So I began studying **Agile software development** deeply—reading the Agile Manifesto, and books from Kent Beck, Martin Fowler, Robert C. Martin.

Then I realized—we weren’t really practicing Agile the way it was meant to be.  
I knew how it could be better—but I couldn’t **convince my manager or leadership team**. That frustrated me.

At the same time, I was beginning to study **Buddhism**, and I came across a quote from a monk who paraphrased the Buddha:

> *"Trying to explain the truth to someone who hasn’t experienced it is like trying to explain colors to a person born blind."*

It struck me deeply.  
The Buddha, too, faced this challenge—**how do you explain what cannot be seen unless experienced?**

It gave me peace—knowing that even the Buddha had to go through that frustration.

And so I realized—**sometimes the best you can do is walk the path, not push others onto it.**

## ❤️ This Is Also for My Wife

My wife endured **9 months of pregnancy**, morning sickness, emotional stress, and physical strain.  
She then went through **delivery**, **healing**, and **nursing a newborn**—while also taking care of our older son.

At the same time, I was often angry and overwhelmed.  
This affected our relationship, and I could see it—yet I didn’t know how to stop.

I wasn’t able to understand her pain, because I was lost in mine.

So this website is not just for me. **It’s for her too.**  
I'm learning so I can support her better.

---

## 🧭 A Path, Not a Destination

This is not a textbook.  
It is not religious marketing.

It’s a **pathway guide**—for those who want to begin the journey to become a Buddha.

I am walking that path.  
If you are too, then maybe we can walk together.
